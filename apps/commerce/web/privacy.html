Aria Commerce Sandbox privacy

This service is an account-restricted Sandbox pilot operated by Arthera. It is not a production payment service.

Information processed

Google authenticates your account using OpenID Connect. We use its stable account identifier to look up an operator-assigned merchant role. The service does not request Gmail or Drive access. Google tokens are not forwarded to connected AI applications.

The service stores catalogue data, order drafts, amounts, workflow events and verified PayPal Sandbox references. Do not submit real card details or sensitive personal, health or financial information in prompts. A selected server-side model receives a drafting prompt only when that drafting mode is configured; host-model drafting sends selected SKUs and quantities instead.

Connected applications

An application receives only the tools and account permissions you approve. Server-side access and renewal credentials are stored as hashes. Your client manages the credential itself; the optional local connector saves it in your own user profile. Connected clients receive an opaque account identifier, products and order data within the assigned merchant. They cannot approve or pay using these tools.

Revocation and retention

Revoke an app under Your connected account → Connected apps to stop its access and renewal immediately. Disconnecting does not delete historical orders, audit events or data already received by a client. Record and infrastructure log retention follow the deployment operator's storage configuration; this pilot does not promise automatic deletion on disconnect. Contact the operator for deletion or export requests.

Service providers and questions

This hosted pilot uses Google Cloud for storage and hosting, Google for sign-in and PayPal for Sandbox payments. Connected AI platforms process tool results according to their own terms and your account settings.

For support, report non-sensitive issues through the project issue tracker. Do not post credentials or personal information in public issues.
